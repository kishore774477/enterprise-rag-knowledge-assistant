# HR Policy Handbook

Orion Dynamics Ltd. — Human Resources. Version 3.2, effective 1 April 2026. Applies to all full-time employees in India unless a local addendum says otherwise.

## Working Hours and Attendance

### Standard Working Hours
The standard work week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday. Core collaboration hours are 10:30 AM to 4:00 PM IST, during which employees are expected to be available for meetings. Outside core hours, employees may choose their start and end times with their manager's agreement.

### Attendance and Timesheets
Employees must record their working time in the HRMS portal by 6:00 PM every Friday. Missing two consecutive timesheets triggers a reminder from the HR Business Partner. Repeated unexplained absence of more than three working days without informing the manager is treated as unauthorised absence.

## Leave Policy

### Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward into the next year; any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 December. Annual leave requests of 3 days or more must be submitted in the HRMS portal at least 14 days in advance and approved by the reporting manager.

### Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward. A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days.

### Casual Leave
Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per year for personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave and cannot be taken for more than 2 consecutive days.

### Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Paternity leave is 10 working days of paid leave, which must be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

### Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday calendar for each office location is published on the intranet every December.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees who have completed their probation may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require approval from the department head and HR. Employees working remotely must use the company VPN and keep their status updated in the collaboration tool during core hours. The company provides a one-time home office allowance of INR 15,000 for ergonomic equipment, claimable through the expense portal.

## Probation and Onboarding
New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. During probation the notice period is 15 days. A probation review is conducted by the reporting manager in the fifth month; probation may be extended once by up to 3 months. Every new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy for the first 90 days.

## Performance Reviews
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Employees are rated on a 5-point scale. Annual salary revisions are effective from 1 July and are based on the April review.

## Benefits

### Health Insurance
All employees are covered by group health insurance with a sum insured of INR 5,00,000 per year. Coverage includes the employee, spouse, up to two children and parents. Employees can buy a top-up policy at group rates during the enrolment window in January.

### Learning and Development
Each employee has an annual learning budget of INR 40,000 for courses, certifications and conferences. Requests must be approved by the manager before enrolment, and the certificate of completion must be submitted to claim reimbursement.

### Employee Referral Bonus
Employees who refer a candidate that is hired receive a referral bonus of INR 50,000, paid after the new hire completes 3 months of service.

## Code of Conduct
Employees must treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are strictly prohibited. Concerns can be reported to the HR Business Partner or anonymously through the Ethics Hotline. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee (IC) under the POSH Act, and an inquiry is completed within 90 days.

Gifts from vendors or customers worth more than INR 5,000 must be declared to the Compliance team within 7 days.

## Resignation and Exit
After probation, the notice period is 60 days for all employees. Notice period buyout is allowed only with approval from the department head. Exit interviews are conducted by HR in the last week of employment. Full and final settlement is processed within 45 days of the last working day.
