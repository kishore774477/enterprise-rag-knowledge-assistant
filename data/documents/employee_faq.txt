Orion Dynamics Ltd. - Employee FAQ (General)

Q: When is salary paid?
A: Salary is credited on the last working day of every month. Payslips are available in the HRMS portal on the same day.

Q: How do I update my bank account details for salary?
A: Raise a request in the HRMS portal under "My Profile > Bank Details" with a cancelled cheque. Changes submitted before the 20th of the month apply to that month's salary.

Q: Where can I download Form 16?
A: Form 16 for the previous financial year is published in the HRMS portal by 15 June every year.

Q: Is there a cafeteria in the office?
A: Yes. The cafeteria on the ground floor serves breakfast from 8:30 AM to 10:30 AM and lunch from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM. Meals are subsidised and paid using the employee ID card.

Q: Does the company provide transport?
A: Shuttle buses run from the main metro station to the Bengaluru office every 20 minutes between 8:00 AM and 10:30 AM, and between 5:00 PM and 8:00 PM.

Q: How do I get a visitor pass for a guest?
A: Register the visitor in the facilities portal at least one day in advance. Visitors must show a government photo ID at reception and be escorted at all times.

Q: Who do I contact for an employment verification letter?
A: Request it through the HRMS portal under "Letters". Employment verification letters are issued within 3 working days.

Q: Is parking available?
A: Car parking is allotted on a first-come, first-served basis through the facilities portal. Two-wheeler parking is free for all employees.
