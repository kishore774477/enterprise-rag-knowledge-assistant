# Finance and Expense Manual

Orion Dynamics Ltd. — Finance Department. Version 2.4, effective 1 April 2026.

## Purpose and Scope
This manual explains how employees spend company money, how expenses are claimed and approved, and how purchases are made. It applies to all employees, contractors and interns who incur expenses on behalf of the company.

## Expense Claims

### Submitting a Claim
All expense claims must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted later than 30 days require written approval from the department head and the Finance Controller. Every claim must include an itemised receipt; for any single expense above INR 500, a GST invoice in the company's name is mandatory.

### Reimbursement Timeline
Approved expense claims are reimbursed to the employee's salary bank account within 10 working days of final approval. Claims approved after the 25th of the month are paid in the next payment cycle.

### Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following are not reimbursed: alcohol, traffic or parking fines, personal entertainment, spa or gym charges, minibar charges, and travel upgrades that were not pre-approved.

## Approval Limits
Expenses are approved according to the following delegation of authority:

- Up to INR 25,000: approved by the reporting manager.
- INR 25,001 to INR 1,00,000: approved by the department head.
- Above INR 1,00,000: approved by the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

No employee may approve their own expenses or the expenses of someone who approves theirs.

## Business Travel

### Booking Travel
All flights, trains and hotels must be booked through the company travel desk at least 7 days before the trip, except in emergencies. Employees travel in economy class for flights of less than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more for employees at Director level and above.

### Hotel Limits
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) and up to INR 4,000 per night in other cities. For international travel, the limit is USD 180 per night.

### Per Diem
Employees on domestic business travel receive a daily allowance (per diem) of INR 1,500 for meals and incidentals. The international per diem is USD 60 per day. Per diem is not paid for the day of travel if the trip is shorter than 8 hours.

### Local Conveyance
Taxi and ride-hailing expenses for business purposes are reimbursed at actual cost. Use of a personal car for business is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre, and a personal two-wheeler at INR 5 per kilometre.

## Corporate Credit Card
Corporate credit cards are issued to employees who travel frequently, with a monthly limit of INR 2,00,000. The card must be used only for business expenses. Cardholders must reconcile and submit card statements in the expense portal by the 5th of every month. Personal use of the corporate card, even if repaid, is a policy violation and may lead to the card being withdrawn.

## Cash Advances
Employees can request a travel cash advance of up to INR 30,000 per trip through the expense portal. Advances must be settled within 15 days of returning from the trip; unsettled advances are recovered from salary.

## Procurement
Purchases of goods or services above INR 50,000 must go through the procurement team using a purchase order (PO). Purchases above INR 5,00,000 require at least three competitive quotations. New vendors must be onboarded and verified by Finance before any PO is issued. Vendor invoices are paid within 45 days of receipt of a valid invoice.

## Budgeting
Department budgets are set annually in March for the financial year April to March. Spending more than 10% above the approved budget for any cost head requires CFO approval.
