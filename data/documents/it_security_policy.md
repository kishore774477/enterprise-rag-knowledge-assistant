# IT and Information Security Policy

Orion Dynamics Ltd. — IT Department. Version 1.8, effective 1 January 2026.

## Passwords and Authentication
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include upper-case letters, lower-case letters, numbers and symbols. Passwords expire every 90 days, and the last 5 passwords cannot be reused. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all cloud applications. Accounts are locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. Never share your password with anyone, including the IT helpdesk.

## Company Devices
Every employee receives a company laptop on joining. Laptops are encrypted with full-disk encryption and managed by the IT department. Employees must not install unlicensed or unapproved software; requests for new software are raised through the IT service portal. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.

## Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
Personal mobile phones may be used to access company email and chat only after enrolling them in the Mobile Device Management (MDM) app. Personal laptops must not be used to access company systems.

## Data Classification
Company information is classified into four levels:

- Public: approved for release outside the company, such as marketing material.
- Internal: for employees only, such as internal announcements.
- Confidential: restricted to specific teams, such as customer contracts and financial reports.
- Restricted: highly sensitive data such as employee salary data, personal data and source code secrets. Restricted data must be encrypted in storage and in transit.

Confidential and Restricted data must not be uploaded to personal cloud storage or public AI tools.

## Email and Internet Use
Company email must be used for business purposes. Do not forward company email to personal accounts. Suspicious emails must be reported using the "Report Phishing" button in the mail client. Access to gambling, adult and file-sharing websites is blocked on the company network.

## Security Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident, such as a phishing click, malware infection, data leak or lost device, must be reported to the Security Operations Centre (SOC) at security@oriondynamics.example or extension 7777 immediately, and in any case within 1 hour of discovery. Do not try to investigate the incident yourself.

## Access Management
Access to systems is granted on a least-privilege basis and requires manager approval through the IT service portal. Access rights are reviewed every quarter. When an employee leaves, all access is revoked on their last working day.

## Backups
Business data on company servers is backed up daily, and backups are retained for 90 days. Employees must store work files on the company OneDrive or SharePoint, not on the local laptop drive.

## IT Helpdesk
The IT helpdesk is available 24x7 at extension 5000 or through the IT service portal. Priority 1 issues (complete outage) have a response time of 30 minutes.
